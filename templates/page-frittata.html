FRITTATA

INGREDIENTS

30 g fresh ginger
1 carrot
1 bell pepper
1 zucchini
1 broccoli
250 g mushrooms
500 g cooked beetroot
70 g black olives
6 eggs
6 tbsp Greek yoghurt

olive oil
smoked paprika
basil · oregano · marjoram
dill · thyme
nutritional yeast
sea salt · black pepper

METHOD

01

STEAM

Vegetables + ginger until tender.

02

SEASON

Olive oil · olives · herbs · spices · sea salt.

03

WHISK

Eggs + Greek yoghurt · herbs · spices · sea salt.

04

POUR

Pour over vegetables. Gently combine.

05

BAKE

190°C · ~26 min until golden.

06

ENJOY

Slice · serve · share.
